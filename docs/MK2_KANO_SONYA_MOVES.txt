MK2 KANO / SONYA - FULL MOVELIST FOR THIS PORT
Includes fatalities, Friendships, Babalities, stage finishes and Shang morphs.

Extract the entire release ZIP and run PLAY.cmd. In the working folder,
PLAY_MK2_KANO_SONYA.cmd also launches the game. Tab opens input settings.

NOTATION
F = Forward, toward the opponent. B = Back, away from the opponent.
D = Down. U = Up. Directions reverse when you switch sides.
HP = High Punch. LP = Low Punch. HK = High Kick. LK = Low Kick.
A comma means the next input in sequence; + means press together.
Enter direction sequences quickly, then press the final attack button.
These commands are specific to this port, not an unmodified MK3 movelist.

KANO - SPECIAL MOVES
Knife Throw:       D, B + HP.
Blade Swipe:       D, F + HP. Close-range swipe, not a projectile.
Choke:             D, F + LP. Close range.
Cannonball:        Hold LK for about 1.5 seconds, then release LK.
Choke cannot grab Kintaro or Shao Kahn.

KANO - FINISHERS
Skeleton Rip:      B, D, F + HK. Close range.
Eye Laser:         LP, Block, Block, HK. Mid-range, roughly half-screen apart.
                   Tap separately; RELEASE Block before the final HK.
                   Do not hold other attack buttons with HK.
Friendship:        LK, Block, Block, HK. Stand well away.
Babality:          F, F, D, D + LK. Stand well away.
Pit II / Tomb:     U, U, B + LK. Close, on the appropriate stage.

SONYA - SPECIAL MOVES
Energy Ring:       D, F + LP.
Bicycle Kick:      B, B, D + HK.
Leg Grab:          Hold D and press LP + Block together. Close range.
Square Wave:       F, B + HP. Use against an AIRBORNE opponent.
Leg Grab and Bicycle Kick cannot grab Kintaro or Shao Kahn.
Square Wave does not damage a grounded opponent.

SONYA - FINISHERS
Kiss of Death:     B, F, D, D + LP. Close-to-mid range; burns the victim.
Death Orb/Bubble:  HOLD BLOCK, then U, U, B, D + HK. Far apart.
                   Keep Block held THROUGHOUT, including the final D + HK.
                   Do not hold HP, LP or LK.
Friendship:        B, F, B, D + Block. Stand well away.
Babality:          D, D, F + LK. Stand well away.
Pit II / Tomb:     F, F, D + HP. Close, on the appropriate stage.

SHANG TSUNG - ADDED MORPHS
Transform to Kano: B, D, B + LK.
Transform to Sonya: D + LP + Block together.
Enter while Shang is grounded and free to act, not jumping or recovering.
Once transformed, use that character's commands above.

SHARED BASIC CONTROLS
Uppercut:          D + HP.
Sweep:             B + LK.
Roundhouse:        B + HK.
Throw:             F + LP at touching distance.
Standing Block:    Block.
Crouching Block:   D + Block.
Dead Pool finish:  During FINISH HIM/HER, uppercut at close range, then hold
                   LP + LK through the launch into the acid.

FINISHING RULES AND TIPS
Fatalities, Friendships, Babalities and stage finishes require FINISH HIM/HER.
For safe Friendship/Babality eligibility, win using kicks only: no HP or LP.
There is NO Run button. Imported commands using Run are adapted to Block.
For commands containing Up, Block can prevent an unwanted jump; Sonya's
Death Orb specifically REQUIRES Block to remain held throughout its command.
Range, blocking, target height and recovery matter: activation is not a hit.
Against Jax's ground pound, jump; blocking while grounded does not stop it.
No persistent hints or overlays are added to the game by this text file.
